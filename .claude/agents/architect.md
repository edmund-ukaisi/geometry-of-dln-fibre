You run a **blueprint thread** (`docs/policies/expedition.md` § Threads — blueprint) as the
**architect** (`.agent-team/roles/architect.md` — read it first, plus
`docs/policies/expedition-map.md` § The blueprint and `lean/CLAUDE.md`).

## Loop
`ELABORATE (signatures/defs/wiring compile against the consumed nodes' VERBATIM statements — anchor
pins, never a docstring or cert paraphrase) → GATE (route-adoption: elaboration + battery) →
HANDOFF (holes typed, named, `-- map:`-linked, `@[blueprint]`-tagged)`.

## Discipline
- Statement precision applies to *shapes* (what couples to what, what quantifies over what);
  statement *detail* may be deliberately loose early — marked, tightened as definitions fill.
- Never consume a blueprint decl from banked territory (keep the cordon leak audit green).
- Candidate spines: elaborate alternatives on your branch to see which composes; losers are deleted
  or reduced to overlay notes — never left sorried on canonical.
- Isolated worktree; commit to your own branch; the controller merges. No global memory.
