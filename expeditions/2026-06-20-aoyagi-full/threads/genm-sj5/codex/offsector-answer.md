1. **FACT:** \(\bigsqcup_{q=1}^r S_q\) equals \(\{\sigma_{\min}(Z)=0\}\), not the positive-measure set \(\{\sigma_{\min}(Z)<\varepsilon\}\). The argument therefore needs measurable tube regions \(T_q\), with \(\{\sigma_{\min}<\varepsilon\}\subseteq\bigcup_qT_q\), obtained for example by classifying how many ordered singular values lie below overlapping cutoffs. **FACT:** Comparable or equal singular values do not inherently escape the ordered simplex, but the density estimate must remain uniform up to multiplicity walls or those walls must receive additional charts. **FACT:** Cutoff seams likewise require overlapping bands or an explicit half-open partition. **JUDGEMENT:** Finitely many coranks do not by themselves provide finitely many uniform tube charts; one also needs compact localization, a finite subcover, or global uniform estimates.

2. **FACT:** Failure of first-order vanishing need not imply a further rank drop. For example, in the scalar matrix-product case \(Z=ab\), the point \(a=b=0\) still has corank \(1\), although \(Z\) vanishes to order \(2\); there is no higher-corank stratum to catch it. **FACT:** Thus the complement of the reduced \(m=1\) part of \(S_q\) may be a same-corank critical locus of the parameter-to-matrix map. **JUDGEMENT:** A valid good tube must impose a uniform transversality/Jacobian bound, while every excluded bad tube sector—not merely its measure-zero centre—must lie in a separately estimated neighbourhood. **FACT:** Recursion by corank works only after proving that this entire bad region is controlled by a strictly higher-corank tube; otherwise the stratification must also record multiplicity or transversality degeneracy and recompute its threshold.

3. **FACT:** For a finite measurable cover by genuinely controlled tubes, nonnegativity gives
\[
\int_{\{\sigma_{\min}<\varepsilon\}}H^{-c'}\,d\lambda
\leq \sum_q\int_{T_q}H^{-c'}\,d\lambda,
\]
so overlap and double-counting cannot invalidate finiteness. **FACT:** Boundary intersections are harmless once the tubes are measurable and the estimates include their neighbourhoods. **JUDGEMENT:** Corank increasing and being bounded proves only termination of the indexing recursion, not that the induction closes analytically. At every level one must verify the same loss lower bound, pushforward-density estimate, uniform constants, and containment of the remaining bad region. **FACT:** Merely having locally finite estimates on infinitely many charts is insufficient without summability or a finite subcover.

COVER-AIRTIGHT: **needs-condition-that every non-\(m=1\) tube sector is contained in a controlled strictly-higher-corank neighbourhood**.
