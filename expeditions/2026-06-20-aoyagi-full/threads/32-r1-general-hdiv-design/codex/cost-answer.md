The math route looks substantially settled: the uniform `phi_M` recipe, `u^2` rate, and determinant shape have been tested across structurally different cases. The Lean route is not just “rerun det tactic at larger N”: growing weighted axes, actual-det-dependent `leafH`, arbitrary chaining choices, and arbitrary `L` mean the proof needs reusable determinant/derivative/cov infrastructure, not more hand expansion.

**Q2. Verdict: Heaviest sub-piece is (ii) the symbolic determinant at general N.**  
The determinant is the load-bearing certificate tying the chart to `leafH p = minAdm - 1`, the monomial threshold, and the `cov` Jacobian weight. `HasFDerivAt` is large, but mostly compositional once the chart is expressed through reusable maps; `cov` is annoying but finite-measure bookkeeping. The determinant needs a general block-triangular/composition proof over fin-indexed path cases, and the facts show the spectator monomial is construction-sensitive, so it cannot be safely faked or globally hand-waved.

**Q3. Verdict: Bedrock move is (a) a single general-M theorem, but only after a design pass.**  
Per-node instantiations will keep proving the same fragile facts with worse constants and will not de-risk arbitrary `M`. The honest unit is a uniform `phi_M` indexed by the achiever/descent data, with general lemmas for derivative, determinant, and finite null-slice cov. Building `(3,3,3,3)` first is useful as a stress test, but not as the final architecture.

**Q4. Verdict: A cheaper lower-bound route may exist mathematically, but it is unlikely cheaper in Lean unless it avoids injective c-o-v entirely.**  
A wedge comparison could aim to prove on a positive-measure box sector that `routeMCore M (psi u) <= C*u^2` with a product-measure set in spectator variables, then integrate `u^{-minAdm}` against a known volume factor. But to get the codimension exponent `minAdm - 1`, one still needs the correct transverse-volume scaling, which is exactly what the chart Jacobian encodes. So unless the alternative proves that scaling by a simpler Fubini/box-volume construction, it just reintroduces the determinant under another name.

**Q5. Net Verdict: NEEDS NEW DESIGN.**  
This is not a research wall: your evidence says the mathematical chart and rate exist, and the banked `NodeAchieverChart -> atom` theorem isolates the remaining obligation cleanly. But it is also not a commission-now “det-tactic tide”: the general case needs a carefully engineered Lean representation of the uniform chart, a general determinant theorem matching the actual construction, and a finite-family cov/null-slice lemma. The single thing most likely to make this verdict too pessimistic is if the closed-form `phi_M` can be encoded as a composition of already-proved elementary maps whose determinant exponents compose automatically, reducing the general determinant to bookkeeping rather than a new block-triangular proof.
