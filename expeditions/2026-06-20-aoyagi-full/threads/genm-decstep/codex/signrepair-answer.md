### Q1 — PROVEN arithmetic

For \(n=5\), the binding cut may be \(u=3\), so \(b=2\) and \(m=0,1,2\). Since

\[
\minAdm(a,5,5)=5a-\left\lfloor\frac{a^2}{4}\right\rfloor,
\]

we obtain:

| \(m\) | \(u'=5-m\) | \(\minAdm(u',5,5)\) | \(T_m\) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 5 | 19 | \(19/2\) |
| 1 | 4 | 16 | \(17/2\) |
| 2 | 3 | 13 | \(17/2\) |

Here \(\minAdm(5,5,5,5)=17\), hence \(c^*=17/2\). Thus every stratum satisfies \(T_m\ge c^*\), with \(m=1,2\) marginal.

More generally this inequality is immediate from the recursion: \(m^2+\minAdm(n-m,n,n)\) is one candidate in the minimum defining \(\minAdm(n,n,n,n)\). The implication for exceptional powers is conditional on this quantity actually being the discrepancy/vanishing-order ratio of the relative resolution.

### Q2 — ordinary fact PROVEN; application here INFERENCE

For a smooth center \(Z\subset X\) of codimension \(d\),

\[
|\det D\pi|=|e|^{d-1}\times(\text{positive unit})
\]

on a blow-up chart. For the exact-rank-\(r\) stratum in \(p\times q\) matrices,

\[
d=(p-r)(q-r).
\]

Thus for square corank \(m\), \(d=m^2\). Strictly, the Jacobian exponent is \(m^2-1\), while the threshold numerator is \(a(E)+1=m^2\).

A smooth relative determinantal family has exactly the same discrepancy. On a pivot chart, writing

\[
R=[R_1\ R_2],\qquad B=[B_1\ B_2],
\]

with \(R_1\) invertible, the quotient coordinate is

\[
S=B_2-B_1R_1^{-1}R_2.
\]

The transformation \((R,B_1,B_2)\mapsto(R,B_1,S)\) is triangular with unit Jacobian. Hence variation of the pivot row space and the orthogonality identity alone do not alter the determinantal codimension.

The unresolved point is that \(R=\widetilde Q_p\) and \(B=Q_b\) are not free relative-matrix coordinates: they are produced by shared deeper factors. The pullback to those variables need not be transverse to the relative rank strata, and resolving \(S\) alone does not principalize the joint ideal generated by \(\Gamma S\). Under a non-submersive pullback, codimension, multiplicity, and discrepancy need not remain the naive \(m^2\). A relative joint-principalization calculation is therefore still required.

### Q3 — no arithmetic sign failure; analytic repair unproved

Conditionally on the claimed normal form, every stratum passes: \(T_m\ge c^*\) implies \(a_i-2c'N_i>-1\) for \(c'<c^*\). Binding strata reach \(-1\) only at \(c'=c^*\).

There is no identified \((n,m)\) where the correctly computed charge gives a numerical failure. The first unresolved analytic case is \(n=4\), \(b=2\), at the intermediate rank-one stratum \(m=1\), where the joint \(\Gamma Q_b^\perp\) incidence first appears. This is an unproved case, not a demonstrated sign failure.

### Q4 — INFERENCE, not formalisation-ready

The arithmetic is a valid certificate for the desired resolution. The ordinary and smoothly relative determinantal Jacobian formula is standard. What is not yet proved is that the shared-tail product supplies smooth transverse quotient coordinates—or, failing that, that its full joint resolution has precisely those discrepancies and no additional exceptional divisors of smaller ratio.

Hence the uniform repair is plausible and buildable, but not established by the standard determinantal fact alone.
