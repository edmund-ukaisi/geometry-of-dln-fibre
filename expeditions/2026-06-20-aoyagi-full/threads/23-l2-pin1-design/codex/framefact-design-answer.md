2. **htop1/htop2:** the clean block chain uses column partitions, not `toBlocks₁₁` on a row-unsplit matrix. Use `Matrix.reindex_apply` + `Matrix.submatrix_mul_equiv` to get `reindex refl e (V*Q) = reindex refl e V * reindex e e Q`; rewrite `reindex refl e V` as `Matrix.fromCols VJ VK`; rewrite `reindex e e Q` by `← Matrix.fromBlocks_toBlocks`; apply `Matrix.fromCols_mul_fromBlocks`; read with `Matrix.toCols₁_fromCols`, `Matrix.toCols₂_fromCols`. This gives the desired left/right expressions, but the `corM` side is not `fromCols 1 0`: its left entries are `if (i:ℕ) = (e.symm (Sum.inl k):ℕ) ∧ (i:ℕ)<r then 1 else 0`. Hence htop1/htop2 follow only when the sorted pivot columns are exactly `0,…,r-1`.

3. **`V.rank = r`:** this part is sound. `V.rank ≤ A.rank` is `Matrix.rank_submatrix_le (Fin.castLE h) (Equiv.refl _) A`. For the reverse, define the zero-extension linear map `ι : (Fin r → ℝ) →ₗ[ℝ] (Fin a → ℝ)`; tail-vanishing proves `A.mulVecLin = ι.comp V.mulVecLin` by `Matrix.mulVecLin_apply`. Then `Matrix.rank`, `LinearMap.range_comp`, and `Submodule.finrank_map_le` give `A.rank ≤ V.rank`. Combine with `A.rank = r`.

4. **Overall:** the design has a real gap in Q2, not just the Q1 permutation. Counterexample: `r=1`, `A = [0 1]`, `Q = ![![0,1],![1,0]]`; then `A*Q = [1 0]`, the only pivot column is `1`, and the pivot-split `Q₂₂` is `0`. To use the keystone, the normal form must be pivot-aligned (`A*Q = corM` with identity in the chosen pivot columns), or `Q` must be specially constructed/postcomposed accordingly.
