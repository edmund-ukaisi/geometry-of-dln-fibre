Q3

**VERDICT: genuine analytic escalation, with an exact strata-min conditional on the sector estimates.** Put
\[
q=c-\frac a2,\qquad
D(t)=(M_0-t)(M_1-t),\qquad
R(t)=\operatorname{minAdm}(\operatorname{redChain}_tM).
\]
A correct sector indexed by \(t\) must yield
\[
q<\frac{D(t)-a+R(t)}2.
\]
Taking all relevant sectors gives
\[
q<\frac{\min_t(D(t)+R(t))-a}{2}
 =\frac{\operatorname{minAdm}(M)-a}{2},
\]
which is exactly equivalent to
\[
c<\frac12\operatorname{minAdm}(M).
\]
The sphere factor costs nothing precisely under the stated hypothesis \(a<u\). Sector logarithms can affect endpoint pole multiplicity, but leave no residual loss in this strict RLCT threshold.
