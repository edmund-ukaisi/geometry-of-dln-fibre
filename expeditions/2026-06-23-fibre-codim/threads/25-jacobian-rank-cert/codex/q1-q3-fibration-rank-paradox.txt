**Q1: TRUE, with hypotheses.**  
Fact: if `mult|Sigma^r : Sigma^r -> Mat_{d_N x d_0}^{<=r}` is dominant and `E` has rank `r`, then

```text
dim mult^{-1}(E) = dim Sigma^r - dim(rank-r stratum)
                 = (card - C) - r(d_0+d_N-r).
```

So the fibre codimension in `Rep_d` is `C + delta`. The `+delta` is not a tangent-space correction; it is the loss from fixing a point inside the rank-`r` stratum after first imposing `rank(mult)<=r`. Need nonempty/dominance onto the rank-`r` stratum, e.g. effectively `r <= min_i d_i`.

**Q2: FALSE as stated; the tangent-containment claim is wrong.**  
Fact: `mult` does **not** land in the determinantal variety `Mat^{<=r}` on a neighborhood of `A`; only the chosen point `A` lies in `Sigma^r = mult^{-1}(Mat^{<=r})`. Therefore

```text
d(mult)_A(T_A Rep_d) ⊆ T_E Mat^{<=r}
```

does not follow.

Correct statement: if one restricts the differential to `T_A Sigma^r`, then its image lies in `T_E Mat^{<=r}`. But the full differential on `T_A Rep_d` may point transversely out of the rank-`<=r` locus. Hence `(2,2,2), r=1` can have rank `4` although `dim T_E Mat^{<=1}=3`.

Inference: on the top fibre component, `rank d(mult)=C+delta` says the fibre equations have expected rank there; it is not bounded by the tangent dimension of the target determinantal locus.

**Q3: NEEDS-HYP.**  
Generic smoothness of the map `mult : Rep_d -> Mat` alone does not force smoothness over a fixed singular-value fibre `E`.

What is enough: if a top-dimensional irreducible component `Z` of `F=mult^{-1}(E)` is generically reduced/smooth of dimension `card-C-delta`, and locally `F` is cut out as the fibre of `mult`, then at a generic smooth point `A in Z`,

```text
dim ker d(mult)_A = dim Z = card-C-delta,
```

so

```text
rank d(mult)_A = C+delta.
```

Thus the rank conclusion is true generically on a generically reduced top component. Lower-dimensional components may have larger differential rank, exactly as your computations show.
