**Q3.** The polynomial-curve route is **higher friction**. The tempting identity `(1 + tφ)⁻¹ = 1 - tφ` is secretly false over `k[t]`; it only holds after killing `t²`, i.e. in dual numbers. To use honest `evalGroupRing_orbitPullback`, you must restrict to parameters where every `det (1 + tφ_v) ≠ 0`, build `P t : BaseChangeGroup k d`, clear denominators, and use an infinite-root argument. Relevant lemmas are `Polynomial.finite_setOf_isRoot`, `Polynomial.eq_zero_of_infinite_isRoot`, local `eval_aeval_curve`, and probably `Polynomial.taylor_coeff_one` if extracting the linear coefficient. That is substantially more bookkeeping than the dual-number proof, which localizes once and gets the inverse exactly by `one_add_eps_mul_one_sub_eps`.
