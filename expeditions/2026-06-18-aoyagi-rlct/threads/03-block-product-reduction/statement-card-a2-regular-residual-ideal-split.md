## Statement Shape

`regularBlockEntryIdeal X F2 F3` is the ideal generated by entries of the three
regular p. 13 block families.  The four-block product-difference ideal splits as

```text
fourMatrixEntryIdeal X F2 F3 D =
  regularBlockEntryIdeal X F2 F3 ⊔ matrixEntryIdeal D.
```

The suffix-state and fixed-base source-rank corollaries apply this split to the
canonical fields

```text
S.Ctop - 1,  -S.B,  lowerLeftBlock S.L,  S.D.
```

## Scope

Algebraic regrouping of scalar matrix-entry ideals only.

## Nonclaims

No analytic ideal/germ transport, regular-suspension chart construction,
coverage, Jacobian compatibility, normal crossings, pole order, or RLCT.  The
residual block `S.D` remains outside the regular-coordinate index.
