# Statement card - A2 matrix-entry ideal transport

> **Claim.** Over a commutative ring, matrix-entry ideals are preserved by
> square determinant-unit left and right multiplication. Also, in the
> four-family entry ideal generated by entries of `X`, `F2`, `F3`, and `D`,
> replacing `D` by `D - F3 * F2` gives the same algebraic ideal.
>

>   `DLNFibre.DLN.Aoyagi.matrixEntryIdeal_mul_left_eq_of_isUnit_det`,
>   `DLNFibre.DLN.Aoyagi.matrixEntryIdeal_mul_right_eq_of_isUnit_det`,
>   `DLNFibre.DLN.Aoyagi.matrixEntryIdeal_mul_le_sup`, and
>   `DLNFibre.DLN.Aoyagi.fourMatrixEntryIdeal_sub_mul_eq`
>   (`lean/DLNFibre/DLN/Aoyagi/EntryIdeal.lean` @ `e612f68`)
> - **Gloss.** These theorems prove elementary algebraic equalities and
>   inclusions of ideals generated by matrix entries. They do not compare
>   analytic germs, losses, normal-crossing certificates, or RLCTs.

>   matrix-entry ideal; entries of a product lie in the ideal generated by the
>   factor entries; the `F3 * F2` term is redundant in the four-family entry
