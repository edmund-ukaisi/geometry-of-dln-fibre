## Pen-And-Paper Coordinate Data

For a finite center `E` and a pivot `p in E`, the elementary selected-entry
chart has coordinates `(u, y)` and sends

```text
x_p = u,
x_i = u * y_i    for i != p.
```

The existing generic Lean object `SelectedEntryChartFamilyData E` records this
formula for every pivot of a finite center.  In Case 1 the finite center is

```text
E = {old exceptional generator} union {row-strip entries}.
```

Lean represents the old exceptional generator by the left `Unit` token and
row-strip entries by right-branch pairs.  The token is only finite
bookkeeping; source validity of the hidden old label remains external.

For the source-displayed Case 1 pivots, the standard selected-entry family
therefore has:

```text
old pivot chart:       old |-> u, other generators |-> u * residual
displayed pivot chart: d_(J+1,J+1) |-> u, other generators |-> u * residual.
```

The generic finite consequences then specialize directly:

```text
selected variable occurs among the transformed center values,
sum x_e^2 = u^2 * (1 + sum y_e^2),
Ideal(transformed center values) = (u).
```

These are finite algebra statements about the standard coordinate formula, not
regularity, coverage, or analytic Jacobian statements.
