# A4 Selected-Entry Principalization and Unit Facts

Status: reproduced elementary finite algebra at the selected-entry regularity
boundary.

## Source Data

Aoyagi's Case 1 and Case 2 blow-up charts select one generator from a finite
center and write every center generator as the selected variable times a
normalised coordinate. The source then applies elementary row and column
operations, denoted here by `P`, `Q`, and `Q^-1`.

This note records only the finite algebra common to those selected-generator
charts. It does not construct the affine blow-up atlas.

## Unitriangular Operations

The row operation has block form

```text
P = [ 1   0 ]
    [ *   I ].
```

The column operation and its displayed inverse have block forms

```text
Q    = [ 1   * ],      Q^-1 = [ 1   * ].
       [ 0   I ]              [ 0   I ].
```

Each is block triangular with identity diagonal blocks. Hence each matrix is a
unit, and its determinant is a unit. This is the finite-matrix regularity fact
needed by the local algebra; it is not a Jacobian calculation for an affine
chart map.

## Center Ideal Principalization

Let `C` be a finite center and choose a pivot `p in C`. In the selected-entry
chart, with selected variable `u`, the transformed center generators are

```text
phi_p(p) = u,
phi_p(i) = u rho_i       for i != p.
```

Therefore every transformed center generator lies in the principal ideal
`(u)`. Conversely, because the selected generator itself maps to `u`, the
principal generator `u` lies in the ideal generated by all transformed center
generators. Thus

```text
( phi_p(i) | i in C ) = (u).
```

The Case 2 specialization uses the residual-block center

```text
{J+1..mu_S} x {J+1..n_(S+1)},
```

with actual-width columns. The Case 1 specialization uses the finite
`case1CenterGenerators` set, including the selected old-variable branch and
the row-strip entries.

## Scope

This checkpoint proves:

- the finite `P`, `Q`, and `Q^-1` operation matrices are units;
- their determinants are units;
- a selected-entry chart principalizes the finite center ideal to `(u)`;
- Case 1 and Case 2 specializations of that ideal equality.

It does not prove:

- affine blow-up atlas construction or chart coverage;
- non-displayed source-order transition formulas;
- chart-produced recurrence or exponent post-data;
- polynomial-coordinate Jacobian formulas or analytic germ invariance;
- source comparability, normal crossings, or RLCT extraction.
