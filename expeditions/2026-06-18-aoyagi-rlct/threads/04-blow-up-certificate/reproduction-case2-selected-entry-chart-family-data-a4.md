## Selected-Entry Chart Data

with pivot `p in center` has chart coordinates `(u, residual)` and sends

```text
p      |-> u,
i != p |-> u * residual(i).
```

In Lean this is the existing `selectedEntryChartMap`.  The new structure
`SelectedEntryChartFamilyData center` records this formula for every pivot in
the finite center by giving:

- a chart-point type for each pivot;
- the selected variable;
- residual coordinates;
- transformed values of every variable;
- the exact equality with `selectedEntryChartMap`.

The standard chart family takes chart points to be pairs `(u, residual)`.


The last item is finite ideal algebra: the ideal generated by the transformed
finite-center values is generated by the selected variable `u`.
