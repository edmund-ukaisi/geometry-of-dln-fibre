## Statement

Lean now exposes paper-facing names for Aoyagi's displayed Case 2 top-left
`Q/P` calculation.  The checkpoint names the normalized source block
`D_chart`, `Q`, `Q^-1`, `D''`, the transported following factor
`C' = Q^-1 C`, and the cleared block `D'''`.

The theorem
`matrixEntryIdeal_case2DisplayedPaperDppp_mul_Cprime_eq_top_of_not_next_cont`
then proves that, if the displayed pivot is valid and the next continuation
bound fails, the matrix-entry ideal of `D''' * C'` is the matrix-entry ideal
of the pivot row of `C'`.

## Proved

- Adding zero bottom rows to a matrix does not change its matrix-entry ideal.
- The displayed Case 2 paper block `D''` agrees with the generic post-`Q`
  block.
- The orientation `C' = Q^-1 C` is pinned by
  `D'' * C' = D_chart^pivot * C`.
- The supplied displayed source-chart `Q/P` identity is available in paper
  notation.
- Under failed next continuation, the lower rows of `D''' * C'` are zero in
  pivot-first coordinates, so the matrix-entry ideal is generated by the top
  row of `C'`.
