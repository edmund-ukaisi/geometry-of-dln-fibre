- PDF p. 5, Definition 1 defines `λ_{w*}(F, φ)` by local integrability and

  `φ(w*) != 0`, Aoyagi suppresses `φ`.
- PDF p. 5 defines the RLCT of an ideal generated by analytic functions
  `F_1, ..., F_m` as the RLCT of `F_1^2 + ... + F_m^2`.
- PDF p. 5, Lemma 1 gives generator/ideal comparison: if `G_i` lie in the ideal
  generated by `F_i`, then the RLCT of the `G` sum-of-squares is bounded by
  the RLCT of the `F` sum-of-squares; equality follows when the ideals agree.

  `K(π(u))` is a monomial with even exponents and the pulled-back density
  times Jacobian is a monomial. The formula is
  `λ = min_j (h_j + 1)/(2 k_j)`, and `θ` is the maximum cardinality of the
  set of indices achieving that minimum over local charts.
