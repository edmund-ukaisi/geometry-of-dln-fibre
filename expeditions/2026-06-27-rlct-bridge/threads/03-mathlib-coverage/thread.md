## Headline

R2's Lean-provable content is **almost entirely banked or directly available in Mathlib** — but it is the **algebraic** smooth-local model (conormal `I/I²` free of rank `c`; regular local ring), **not** the rlct value. The `rlct = c/2` step is irreducibly **analytic over ℝ** and stays **Cited**. The decisive design correction (Codex-corroborated): the cited interface must be stated **over ℝ with a real smooth point and Jacobian rank `c`**, not over the alg-closed `k` where the banked algebraic smoothness lives. The "c equations form a **regular sequence (lci)**" framing in the brief is a partial red herring: a *literal* length-`c` regular sequence is a sub-wall (no Koszul/lci in Mathlib), and it is **not needed** for the upper-bound — the conormal-free statement does the algebraic work.

## The R2 build route + its size

**Read (what R2's Lean part actually is).** R2's geometric upper-bound content, stated honestly, is the **algebraic smooth-local model**: at a smooth point of the fibre, the conormal `I/I²` is free of rank `c` (equivalently `dF` / Jacobian has rank `c`). This is **NOT an rlct value** — `rlct = c/2` is real-analytic (Morse-Bott / constant-rank normal coordinates for `K = Σ Fᵢ²`) and stays Cited. The brief's "regular sequence (lci) → quadratic model → rlct = c/2" telescopes an analytic step into the algebraic one; the Lean-provable slab stops at the conormal/Jacobian-rank statement.

**Route (cleanest), three rungs:**

1. **Conormal-free at the smooth point — rank `c`.** Two sub-options, both bounded-but-new:
   - (a) Reuse the banked standard-smooth chart at the smooth point (the one underlying `smooth_point_isRegularLocalRing`), package it as a `SubmersivePresentation`, and invoke Mathlib's **`free_cotangent`** ⟹ `I/I²` free on `c = #relations` classes. Bounded **iff** the banked proof already *constructs/exposes* the chart's submersive presentation; if it only proves abstract smoothness through equivalences, extracting a usable presentation is **painful bookkeeping** (Codex Q2).
   - (b) Bypass presentations: use the banked **`finrank_cotangentSpace_eq_finrank_ker_jacobian`** (`Core/CotangentJacobian.lean`) — at the smooth point, `dim CotangentSpace = ringKrullDim = ambient − c` (banked `finrank_cotangentSpace_eq_of_isSmoothAt`), and rank–nullity on the Jacobian gives `rank(Jacobian) = c`, i.e. the cokernel/conormal has rank `c`. This is the **lower-risk** rung — the Jacobian bridge is already banked over `k`; it needs only the smoothness ⟹ Jacobian-rank-`c` reading, which the cotangent comparison already supplies. **Bounded.**

2. **Restate over ℝ at a real smooth point (the load-bearing correction).** The banked smooth-block lives over alg-closed char-0 `K` (`[IsAlgClosed k]`). The cited analytic theorem needs a **real** map `F : ℝ^N → ℝ^c`, a **real** point on `F⁻¹(0)`, and Jacobian rank `c` *there*. Producing a real smooth point of the real fibre with the right Jacobian rank is **net-new** and is the genuine R2 risk (not the algebra). Likely it must be folded INTO the refined cited interface (state the interface over ℝ, consuming "∃ real smooth pt with `rank dF = c`" as the proved geometric INPUT).

3. **The refined cited interface consumes #1+#2.** Replace `cited_aoyagi_dln` by: `rlct(Σ Fᵢ²) = c/2` GIVEN [real smooth pt + Jacobian rank `c` + the `rlct_global ≤ rlct_local` reduction]. The proved geometry supplies the bracket; the analytic Morse-Bott normal-form-of-a-rank-`c`-submersion-squared stays Cited.

**Size verdict:**
- Conormal-free / Jacobian-rank-`c` over `k` (rung 1b): **bounded-but-new** (reuses banked `CotangentJacobian` + `SmoothPointRegular`). Rung 1a depends on whether the chart is exposed — **bounded or painful-bookkeeping**.
- Literal length-`c` **regular sequence** via Koszul/lci: **SUB-WALL** — Mathlib has none of it; bespoke commutative algebra (Codex Q3). **Not needed** for the upper bound; `ringKrullDim_add_length_eq…` would consume one *if* produced, but the conormal-free route sidesteps it.
- Real smooth-point + Jacobian-rank-`c` over ℝ (rung 2): **net-new, the real R2 risk** — the realness/presentation gap, not the algebra (Codex Q4 + biggest-risk).

**Missing rungs (named):**
1. A `SubmersivePresentation` of the DLN fibre at the smooth point (rung 1a) OR the smoothness ⟹ `rank(Jacobian) = c` reading off `CotangentJacobian` (rung 1b). [bounded / bounded-but-new]
2. A **real smooth point** of the real fibre `mult⁻¹(B) ⊆ Rep_ℝ` with `rank dF = c` there. [net-new]
3. The refined `RlctInterface` re-stated over ℝ: `rlct(Σ Fᵢ²) = c/2` from [real smooth pt + Jacobian rank `c`] + the `rlct_global ≤ rlct_local` convention. [interface design — overlaps R0/thread 01]

## Surprises / corrections logged

- **The conormal-rank-`c` statement (S2b) is ALREADY a Mathlib lemma** (`SubmersivePresentation.free_cotangent`), not a build. The banked `fibre_smoothBlock_certificate` deliberately reports the *complementary* module (`Ω`, rank `ambient − c`) and flags in its own docstring that `I/I²` is "a different module" — so the conormal side was left on the table and IS reachable.
- **The brief's "the `c` defining equations form a regular sequence" hides two things:** (i) the loss is `Σ` of `d_N·d_0` entry-squares, NOT `c` squares — `c` is the *height*, not the generator count; "the c equations" can only mean a minimal/regular system at the smooth point. (ii) The regular-sequence/Koszul step does not prove the rlct value (analytic); it would only re-prove the dimension drop the codim result already has. So it is neither literally the entry-functions nor load-bearing for R2.
- **The real↔complex seam is the actual R2 wall**, not the lci/Koszul gap. The banked geometry is over `K` alg-closed; the cited analytic theorem is over ℝ. R0/thread-01 (interface design) and R2 must agree on stating the cited interface over ℝ with the real-smooth-point + Jacobian-rank-`c` datum as the proved INPUT.
