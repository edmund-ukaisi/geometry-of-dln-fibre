**Q1**
**NOT a clean citation** for (R) exactly as stated. The standard theorems do not directly say “the scheme cut out by the single long product is generically reduced along its top components.”

**PROVEN:** (R) is nevertheless a short rank-stratification/Jacobian lemma for this exact scheme. For prefix ranks
\[
r_i=\operatorname{rank}(B_i\cdots B_1),\quad r_0=e_0,\ r_N=0,
\]
the stratum with ranks \(r\) is irreducible of codimension
\[
c(r)=\sum_{i=1}^N (r_{i-1}-r_i)(e_i-r_i).
\]
Top components come from rank sequences minimizing \(c(r)\). At a generic point of such a stratum, the Jacobian rank is
\[
\sum_i (r_{i-1}-r_i)\min_{j\ge i}(e_j-r_j),
\]
with \(e_N-r_N=e_N\). Minimality forces every actual rank drop to occur at a suffix minimum, so this equals \(c(r)\). Hence the generic tangent dimension equals the component dimension. This proves (R), but I would cite it as an internal lemma, not as a named literature theorem.

**Variety of complexes:** **KNOWN-THEOREM, but different.** The Buchsbaum-Eisenbud/Musili/De Concini-Strickland variety of complexes is cut out by all adjacent equations \(D_{i+1}D_i=0\); it is known reduced and its irreducible components are normal. ([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1504.00339)) It equals your \(Z\) only for \(N=2\) (or degenerate zero-dimension cases where the adjacent equations are all vacuous). For \(N\ge3\) with all \(e_i>0\), it is a proper closed subset of \(Z\).

**Type-A orbit closures:** **KNOWN-THEOREM, but not enough.** Type-A quiver orbit closures/quiver loci are normal, Cohen-Macaulay, and have rational singularities via Zelevinsky/Kazhdan-Lusztig models. ([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/1307.6261)) Your \(Z\) is a union of such orbits/orbit closures selected by the one condition \(\operatorname{rank}(B_N\cdots B_1)=0\), not one orbit closure in general. Those theorems do not by themselves prove that the ideal generated by the long-product entries is radical or generically smooth along the union’s top components.

For \(N>2\), I would mark “global reducedness of the single-long-product ideal” as **NOT A CLEAN CITATION / OPEN-FOR-THIS-PURPOSE**: the standard component classification for acyclic truncated path algebras applies to \(\Lambda=kA_{N+1}/J^N\), but it classifies components/generic modules rather than proving scheme-theoretic radicality of the long-product ideal.
