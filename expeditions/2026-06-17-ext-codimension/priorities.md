# priorities.md — taste ledger (ext-codimension)

- **Standard over bespoke.** Each layer must be the textbook development of its object; non-standard
  construction is a smell. Codex-checked.
- **Right generality.** Build at the natural altitude (path algebra / hereditary), where the statement
  is clean — not minimised to our case, not inflated past where it stays standard.
- **Two views, honest bridge.** Variety (`Tuple`) and modules (`kQ`-Mod) kept distinct; the equivalence
  is proved, not fudged; Voigt named as what it is.
- **Proved by development, not cited by default.** A Cited interface only where standard mathematics is
  genuinely out of reach, named in place.
