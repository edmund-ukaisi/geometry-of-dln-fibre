<background>
Fix widths M = (M0, M1, ..., ML). Variables: the entries of L generic matrices C^(s)
(size M_{s-1} x M_s). Let P = C^(1) C^(2) ... C^(L) (size M0 x ML) and let I = <entries of P>
be the ideal generated by the M0*ML product entries, in the local ring at the origin.

F = ||P||^2 = sum of squares of the entries. The real log canonical threshold (rlct) of F at
0 equals lct of the ideal I. For a divisorial valuation v centered at 0, the ratio is
    rho(v) = A_v / (2 v(I)),   A_v = log discrepancy (over the smooth ambient),  v(I)=min_g v(g).
rlct = inf over divisorial v of rho(v). Watanabe's universal bound gives rlct <= (1/2) codim V(I).

There is an integer quantity minAdm(M) (a recursion over rank profiles). It is a THEOREM-analogue
(verified here by an independent Jacobian-rank computation, exact over Q) that codim V(I) = minAdm(M)
exactly, for every instance I tested: (2,2,2),(2,2,3),(3,3,4),(2,2,2,2),(2,3,2,2),(4,4,4,4).
So Watanabe gives rlct <= (1/2) minAdm. The claim under attack is the matching lower bound
rlct >= (1/2) minAdm, i.e. NO divisorial valuation has rho(v) < (1/2) minAdm. Finding one kills it.

For a birational chart phi: (new coords y) -> (old matrix entries x), and a monomial weight w on
y, the induced valuation has (exact change-of-variables / relative-canonical rule)
    2 rho = ( sum_i w_i + w(det d phi/d y) ) / ( min over generator-monomials of w . exponent ),
where w(.) is the min weighted degree over the sympy-EXPANDED (cancellation-corrected) support.
</background>
