<setup>
Loss K(w) = ||A||_F^2, A = C^4 C^3 C^2 C^1 a chain of matrix factors (dims (3,3,3,2,2), so

The oracle emits ONE canonical pivot per node (no fan), so the emitted atlas covers only a "canonical
cone" C_can (argmax pivot canonical at EVERY node). The full cover is recovered as the orbit of C_can
under the loss's symmetry group G.

G is generated by: row-perms of C^1, col-perms of C^4, and INTERNAL "gauge" permutations
C^(s) -> G_{s-1} C^(s) G_s^{-1} with G_s a permutation matrix of the internal index d_s (these cancel
in the product A, so preserve K). Each generator acts on w-space as a COORDINATE PERMUTATION (isometry).

The cover reduces to a stabilizer induction: canonicalize pivots along a root->leaf path one node at a
time; at each node the stabilizer of the already-fixed pivots must (i) act on the next residual block
as a coordinate-permutation (isometry) EQUIVARIANTLY, and (ii) act as the full symmetric group on that
block (so it canonicalizes the next pivot). At a LAYER-ROLLOVER the pivot's row index is one internal
index d_{s-1} and its column index is the next internal index d_s, so BOTH gauge perms G_{s-1}, G_s
are involved -- they COMPOUND. This is the step a depth-2 base case does not exercise.
</setup>
