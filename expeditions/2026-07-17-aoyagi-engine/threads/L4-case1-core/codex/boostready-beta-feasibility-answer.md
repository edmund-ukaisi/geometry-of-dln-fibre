1. VERDICT: **NO-NEEDS-INDUCTION.**

From the stated hypotheses, `hslot` records only membership in the ideal generated by `support`. The goal requires membership in the smaller ideal generated by `center`. Canonical shears do not, merely by being canonical, imply that ideal containment.

This does not establish **NO-NEEDS-BASE**: BOOSTREADY remains valid for arbitrary `e` if the missing b-chain property is supplied as a carried/path-history hypothesis. A canonFlatten base is needed only if one wants to derive that stronger invariant from the root.

2. THE KEY QUESTION: **No.**

For `q ∈ extraBlock`, the counterexample shape is
\[
F(u)=u_q,\qquad c_q(u)=1,\quad c_i(u)=0\ (i\ne q).
\]
This satisfies `hslot` with continuous coefficients. Setting every center coordinate to zero leaves `F(u)=u_q`, which need not vanish.

`hbranch` fixes the coordinate transformations, but no stated hypothesis says that their pullback forces
\[
c_q(u)=u_{\mathrm{pivot}}\beta_q(u).
\]
The witnesses in `hslot` are arbitrary, and canonicality alone is not a divisibility theorem.

Strictly, the concrete `F=u_q` must also be realizable by the actual `coreGen ∘ pathMap` for some `e` to be a counterexample to the fully expanded Lean theorem. Whether it is realizable is unverified. But excluding it would require an additional structural theorem about `canonNormalizationOf`, `coreGen`, and arbitrary `e`; no such property is stated.

3. MINIMAL EXTRA INGREDIENT

Carry the b-chain split itself:
\[
F_j(u)=
\sum_{i\in\mathrm{partialBlock}}\alpha_{j,i}(u)u_i
+
u_{\mathrm{pivot}}
\sum_{i\in\mathrm{extraBlock}}\beta_{j,i}(u)u_i,
\]
with all coefficients continuous.

Then `Deg1SupportedOn center` follows by taking the pivot coefficient to be
\[
c'_{\mathrm{pivot}}(u)=
\sum_{i\in\mathrm{extraBlock}}\beta_{j,i}(u)u_i.
\]

It is enough to assume this at the earlier birth-clear of `f` together with preservation lemmas through intervening steps. That keeps the theorem universally quantified over `e`: for unsuitable `e`, the stronger hypothesis may simply be false.

Without strengthening the carried invariant, the only alternatives are logically equivalent missing inputs: an explicit birth/path-history hypothesis, or a proven ideal-contraction theorem saying that canonical path maps turn `hslot` into the b-chain split. There is no derivation from the stated hypotheses alone.

Logical necessities: `hslot` alone does not encode pivot divisibility; the displayed stronger invariant suffices; some hypothesis or theorem carrying that information is required.
