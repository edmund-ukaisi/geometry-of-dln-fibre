## 1. STEP-BY-STEP AUDIT

- **Step 1 — VALID.** Dropping nonnegative squares gives
  \[
  \mathrm{loss}\ge v_m^2\sum_{k\in S}v_{f_k}^2,
  \]
  so monotonicity gives the claimed RLCT lower bound.

- **Step 2 — VALID, with a wording correction.** The \(z_i\) are not independent of the other coordinates: for example \(z_5=v_{f_1}\) depends on \(u_1\). What matters is that the full map
  \[
  \Psi(u)=(z_1,\ldots,z_8,\text{all unreplaced coordinates})
  \]
  leaves \(u_1,u_5,u_{20}\) unchanged. Its Jacobian has block form
  \[
  \begin{pmatrix}A&B\\0&I\end{pmatrix},
  \qquad
  A=\begin{pmatrix}I_4&0\\u_{10}I_4&I_4\end{pmatrix},
  \]
  hence \(\det D\Psi=1\). Dependence on \(u_1\) occurs only in \(B\), so it neither changes the determinant nor the weight \(u_1^{10}u_5^7u_{20}^8\). In the new coordinates, the comparator really is \(v_m(x)^2|z|^2\).

- **Step 3 — VALID.** On a product neighborhood, ignoring the harmless remaining variables,
  \[
  \int x^\kappa v_m(x)^{-2\lambda}\,dx
  \int_{\mathbb R^r}|z|^{-2\lambda}\,dz
  \]
  converges precisely when
  \[
  \lambda<\min_t\frac{\kappa_t+1}{2(v_m)_t}
  \quad\text{and}\quad
  \lambda<\frac r2.
  \]
  Thus
  \[
  \operatorname{rlct}\bigl(v_m^2|z|^2\bigr)
  =\min\!\left(\operatorname{threshold}(v_m^2),\frac r2\right).
  \]
  A general open transformed neighborhood can be sandwiched between product boxes.

## 2. TORIC DIRECTION

Assertion **(b) is correct**. Let \(I=\langle g_k\rangle\) and let \(M\) be generated by every monomial appearing in the \(g_k\). Then \(I\subseteq M\), and
\[
\sum_k g_k^2\le C\sum_{m\in M}m^2.
\]
Consequently
\[
\operatorname{rlct}(I)\le \operatorname{rlct}(M).
\]
The toric LP therefore gives an **upper bound** on the true RLCT. A dual certificate \(\mathrm{LP}_{\min}\ge8\) only says this upper bound is at least \(4\); it cannot prove the true RLCT is at least \(4\).

Survivor charts are unaffected: if some residual factor is a unit, then \(\sum v_{f_k}^2\) is bounded above and below by positive constants, so the loss is comparable to \(v_m^2\). Equivalently, locally \(I=M=\langle v_m\rangle\), and the monomial threshold is exact.

## 3. HOLES

The most likely hole is checking only independence of eight differentials without verifying that the invertible Jacobian minor uses eight \(\kappa=0\) coordinates while every weighted/\(v_m\)-support coordinate is retained unchanged.

For the displayed chart, that verification succeeds. The \(u_1\)-dependence of \(v_{f_1}\) does not invalidate it. Hence the lower bound is sound:
\[
\operatorname{rlct}(\mathrm{loss})
\ge\min(4,4)=4.
\]

The single-term bound indeed gives only \(1/2\).

## 4. GENERALITY

For every over-vanishing chart it suffices that:

- there are \(r\ge8\) selected residuals and \(r\) distinct \(\kappa=0\) coordinates, disjoint from \(\operatorname{supp}(v_m)\), whose Jacobian minor at \(0\) is invertible; and
- \(\operatorname{threshold}(v_m^2)\ge4\).

Then these residuals form regular coordinate functions, and
\[
\operatorname{rlct}(\mathrm{loss})
\ge\min\!\left(\operatorname{threshold}(v_m^2),\frac r2\right)\ge4.
\]
Thus the argument covers all 16 leaf-types once those two chartwise facts are verified.
