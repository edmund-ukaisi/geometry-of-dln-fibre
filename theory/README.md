# theory/

Markdown theory workspace — the cheap-to-revise substrate where the DLN-fibre / type-A quiver
theory develops before it is formalised. Expedition explore threads write here and in their
expedition dirs; a stable claim graduates to a Lean formalisation thread.

- [`setup.md`](setup.md) — the core objects (the space $\operatorname{Rep}_{\underline d}$ of
  composable matrix tuples, the multiplication map $\operatorname{mult}$, the product-rank loci
  $\Sigma^r$ and the fibres $\operatorname{mult}^{-1}(B)$, and the type-A quiver translation),
  distilled from the paper into the harness's conventions. The first expedition fleshes this out and
  formalises from here.

The paper is the primary source:
`../paper-sources/lehalleur-rimanyi-2024-geometry-of-dln-fibre/source/main.tex` (with the PDF
alongside). The reader-facing digest is
`../docs/expositions/paper-digest/high-level-overview.md`.
