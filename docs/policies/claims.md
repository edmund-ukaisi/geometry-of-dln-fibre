# Claims (absorbed 2026-07-15 — forwarding stub)

The claim system is now the **expedition-map**: [`expedition-map.md`](expedition-map.md).
The card fields became node fields; the statuses became the claim ladder (`conjectured` →
`adjudicated` → `stated` → `skeleton-linked` → `proven`, exits `refuted`/`superseded`/`retired`);
the **two-tier discipline** survives as `tier: new | established` (a new claim's adjudication is the
full stress-test; an established claim's is the light verify-against-source); **kill-conditions are
executable** — battery scripts, not sentences (P1); **refuted claims stay** as nodes with their
witnesses, and the battery re-tests every open route against them for free. Tide-eligibility and
the AUDIT gate: [`expedition.md`](expedition.md) § Maturity, § Gates.
