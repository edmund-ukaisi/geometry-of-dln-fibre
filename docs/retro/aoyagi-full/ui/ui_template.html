Expedition pulse commits.json · H

Commits per day, split by what they are: build (feat/fix/refactor — Lean landing), coordination (ledger/docs/ticks — the controller's shared-memory writes), and other (merges, wip, setup). The coordination band dominating is not overhead gone wrong — it is the price of a 3-week multi-agent proof staying coherent; the knowledge-debt section below measures where that price stops paying.

Where the lines went commits.json · H

Left: cumulative lines added — Lean vs expedition prose. Right: the docs:lean daily ratio, the coordination-share curve. The lifetime average is 1.7 prose lines per Lean line; the ratio climbs in the endgame (adjudication-heavy phases), the measured signature of the burden-of-knowledge effect.

The error economy events.json flags · H (keyword-grade)

Ledger blocks per day carrying correction-class keywords (corrected / retracted / refuted / WALL) vs blocks recording landings. Keyword-grade: a block discussing a correction counts once — event-grade classification is the planned semantic pass. The table lists the longest-lived corrected claims among pairs where the correction names its target (median catch latency of that subsample: under an hour — biased fast, since quick self-corrections cite targets and long sagas often don't).

Knowledge debt knowledge_debt.md · H⁻

"Found already banked" events: a theorem the expedition needed, already proven in its own tree, unfound. Debt hours = how long the artifact sat banked before a lane physically rediscovered it. The double mnp row is the sharpest datum in the whole retro: the same theorem was independently rediscovered twice, ~11 hours apart, by different lanes — the index failure repeating after it had already been exposed once. This table is the empirical case for the librarian role.

Structure: what the theorem stands on decls.json (kernel) · H

From the compiled Lean environment: every declaration, its statement, and the exact constants its proof uses. The cone is the transitive closure of what the headline theorems actually depend on — the load-bearing fraction of the tree. Sorries-on-cone is the kernel-grade integrity number: sorried declarations may exist in dead scaffolding, but zero of them are under any headline. Fan-in ranks the de-facto API — what everything else reaches for. (Snapshot anchor may lag the moving branch; regenerated at each walker run.)
